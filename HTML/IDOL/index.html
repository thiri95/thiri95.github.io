Description

Early Life and education

Julia Roberts was born on October 28, 1967, in Smyrna, Georgia, USA. She grew up in a family with a strong interest in acting and theater. After finishing high school, she moved to New York City to pursue a career in acting.

Career

Julia Roberts made her breakthrough in the romantic comedy Pretty Woman (1990), which became a worldwide success. She later starred in many popular films including My Best Friend's Wedding, Notting Hill, and Erin Brockovich. Her performance in Erin Brockovich earned her the Academy Award for Best Actress in 2001. She is regarded as one of Hollywood's most successful and influential actresses.
